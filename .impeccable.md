## Design Context

### Users
Swob's landing page is for HN, V2EX, and GitHub developers who use multiple AI coding agents heavily. They arrive with little patience and need to decide in seconds whether Swob solves a real problem, whether its claims are technically credible, and whether installing a local macOS app is worth the trust cost.

### Brand Personality
Technical, candid, and quietly opinionated. Swob should feel like a tool built by someone who has personally lost track of hundreds of sessions—not like an enterprise AI platform or a generic SaaS template. The emotional goal is recognition ("this is my mess"), followed by relief and trust.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial developer tooling: warm paper surfaces, ink-like text, precise mono annotations, and branch/registry motifs. Use one rare green accent and real product evidence. Avoid AI-purple gradients as a page-level motif, excessive glass cards, generic startup illustrations, enterprise jargon, and any visual that implies the roadmap lineage-tree UI already ships.

### Design Principles
1. Make the product legible in ten seconds: what it is, why existing viewers miss the problem, and where to download it.
2. Lead with the unique object—cross-session lineage—then reveal search, resume, compact expansion, and token insights as consequences.
3. Prefer evidence over claims: one sanitized product image, one honest dataset, and exact platform names.
4. State limitations plainly without making them the page's dominant emotional ending.
5. Never publish screenshots containing private transcripts, project names, credentials, or machine-specific paths.
6. Keep the page static, fast, keyboard-friendly, responsive, and respectful of reduced motion.
