## Design Context

### Users

Swob serves heavy AI users — developers, writers, researchers, and anyone who works across multiple AI harnesses. They treat their AI conversations as work assets and evidence, not just logs: a home for every conversation they have paid for in time and money. They use Swob to keep, organize, and search that history; recover conversations even after upstream cleanup; audit what each session cost and produced; and resume the correct session without guessing which client or command owns it.

### Brand Personality

Calm, precise, evidence-led. Swob should feel like a trustworthy local instrument: technically capable without being theatrical, explicit about uncertainty, and respectful of user-controlled data.

### Aesthetic Direction

A dense but quiet desktop utility that follows the existing light/dark semantic token system. Hierarchy comes from typography, spacing, alignment, and restrained purple accents—not gradients, decorative cards, oversized icons, or invented colors. Controls favor progressive disclosure and compact, scannable rows suitable for long-lived settings work.

### Design Principles

1. State capability truthfully: opening an app, running a command, and resuming an exact session are visibly distinct.
2. Preserve context: advanced settings are progressively disclosed, while common choices remain immediately scannable.
3. Reuse the project design system: existing semantic colors, seven-step type scale, and spacing rhythm are the source of truth.
4. Make safety visible: unsupported or conditional actions explain why, and unknown capability fails closed.
5. Work across real desktop constraints: light/dark themes, keyboard focus, reduced motion, narrow windows, and independently scrollable panels all remain usable.
