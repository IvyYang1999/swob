## Design Context

### Users
Swob serves power users who work across Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode, and Zcode and need to recover, understand, search, and resume a large local history of AI coding sessions. In the Session Galaxy, their job is to see real lineage, recognize source and project neighborhoods, and jump to a session without losing context.

### Brand Personality
Precise, calm, and trustworthy. Swob should feel like a native developer tool: information-dense without noise, technically capable without spectacle, and explicit about what is a verified relationship versus a visual grouping.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined, utilitarian graph exploration inspired by Obsidian's responsive spatial behavior while remaining visually consistent with Swob's zinc-based light/dark themes and muted purple brand accent. Motion should make the topology legible: an initial expansion, anchored zooming, inertial panning, and elastic node drag. Avoid neon sci-fi styling, decorative glow, fake edges, generic dashboard cards, and motion that obscures data.

### Design Principles
1. Truth before density: draw edges only for verified lineage; express source and project affinity through position, color, or hulls.
2. Motion explains state: use animation for layout formation, navigation, and drag feedback, then let the renderer sleep.
3. Progressive detail: show topology at a distance and reveal labels, neighbors, and metadata on zoom or focus.
4. Local-first responsiveness: keep expensive simulation off the UI thread and make the first frame visible within 500 ms.
5. Accessible by default: support both themes, keyboard focus where applicable, readable contrast, and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
